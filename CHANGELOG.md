## Project update - November 5, 2026

The project is now public under the [MIT license](LICENSE). The app itself did not change, so there is no new installer.

## 1.1.0 - November 5, 2026

### Added
- **English version.** Switch between English and Russian with the RU and EN buttons at the top of the sidebar. The choice is saved.
- **+ Add channel** in the channel list. A new channel is selected automatically after connecting.
- This GitHub page in two languages and this list of changes.

## 1.0.0 - November 5, 2026

First version.

- Build a video from a beat and a picture, with a blurred background, and upload it to YouTube.
- Title, description, tags, playlists, captions, thumbnail and scheduled publishing.
- Tags from prozavr and RapidTags in a hidden browser, remembered for 7 days.
- Auto-fill from the file name, photo search on the web and Pinterest.
- Title options with stats on which one works best.
- Shorts from the loudest part of the beat.
- Automatic first comment, video and channel stats, replies to comments.
- Windows installer.
